How is the flux calculated?

Φnet = qenclosed / ε₀

Outside charges do not contribute to net flux. Zero flux does not necessarily mean zero electric field.

Numerical example

A Gaussian surface encloses a total charge of +3.0 nC. Find the net electric flux.

1. q = +3.0 × 10−9 C
2. Φ = q / ε₀ = (3.0 × 10−9) / (8.854 × 10−12)

Φ ≈ +339 N·m²/C (outward)
