Net flux through each surface

Φnet = qenclosed / ε0

S1 · Calculation

  1. Find enclosed charges.
  2. Add their signed values.
  3. Divide by ε₀.

ε₀ = 8.854 × 10−12 C²/(N·m²). Positive net flux means outward; negative means inward.

What does this experiment demonstrate?

  • Only charges inside a closed surface count toward its net flux.
  • A positive enclosed charge contributes positive flux; a negative one contributes negative flux.
  • External charges can alter the field at different locations on the surface, but their total net flux contribution is zero.
  • If qenclosed = 0, the net flux is zero. It does not mean the electric field is zero everywhere.
  • Gauss' law works for any closed surface, including irregular ones—not only spheres.
