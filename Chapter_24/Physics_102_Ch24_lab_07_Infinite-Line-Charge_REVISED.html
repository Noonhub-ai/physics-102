Calculate it in four short steps

    Important: For a negative line charge, E points inward and flux is negative. The outward surface normal does not reverse when the charge changes sign.

    Discover the physics

    Curved side: E parallel to outward normal (λ positive)θ = 0°
    Curved side: E opposite outward normal (λ negative)θ = 180°
    Top and bottom: E perpendicular to cap normalsθ = 90°
    Curved surface areaA = 2πrL
    Electric field magnitude|E| = |λ|/(2πε₀r)
    Try this: Increase r. The electric field gets weaker, but the curved side becomes larger. Their product, with the correct sign, remains λL/ε₀. Then increase L: the enclosed charge and flux both increase proportionally.

    Predict → test → explain

    1. You double the cylinder radius but keep λ and L fixed. What happens to the net flux?
    2. Which surface has zero electric flux for an ideal infinite line charge?
    3. Calculate: λ = +3 nC/m and L = 2.0 m. What is the net flux?
