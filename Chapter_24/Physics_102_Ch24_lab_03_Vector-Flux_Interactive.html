    5 · What to remember

    The same flux can be calculated either by the dot product of components or by EA cos θ where θ is the angle from E to the selected normal. A field parallel to the surface produces zero flux. A reversed normal reverses the flux sign without changing its magnitude.

    This is flux through an open surface, not the net flux through a closed Gaussian surface. We'll apply Gauss' law to closed surfaces in later laboratories.
