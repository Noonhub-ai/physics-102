Experiment: does distance change E?

Drag the point along the graph or adjust the probe distance slider. The graph shows the electric field's signed component away from the plane on either side. Its magnitude does not depend on distance.

—

Short calculation

    Key idea: Increasing the pillbox length changes neither the enclosed charge (σA) nor the flux. Increasing its radius changes the cap area and thus the enclosed charge and net flux, but not the electric field.
