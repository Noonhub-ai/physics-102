Calculation for your settings

    For the electric field calculation, spherical symmetry is essential. The Gaussian surface is imaginary; the orange solid sphere contains the charge.

    Electric field versus distance

    Drag the point on the graph

    The vertical axis shows the signed radial field: positive means outward, negative means inward. The dashed vertical line marks the physical sphere's surface, r = a. The dot tracks the Gaussian radius.

    Discover the physics

    • Inside (r < a): only charge within radius r is enclosed. E grows linearly with r.
    • At the surface (r = a): the field magnitude is largest for nonzero uniform charge density.
    • Outside (r > a): all the sphere's charge is enclosed. E falls as 1/r².
    • Flux: outside the physical sphere, increasing Gaussian radius changes the area and electric field but not total flux.
    Inside: Er = ρr / (3ε0)
    Outside: Er = ρa³ / (3ε0r²)

    Er is signed. Its magnitude is |Er|. This model assumes uniform volume charge density in vacuum.
