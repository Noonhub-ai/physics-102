Which equation?

k = 8.99 × 10⁹ N·m²/C² · ε₀ = 8.854 × 10⁻¹² F/m · Uniform charge distributions in vacuum. The sheet and line are ideal infinite models.

Compare the four cases

Charge distributionInside / nearOutside / farther awayField behavior
Uniform solid insulator spherer < a: |E| = k|Q|r/a³r ≥ a: |E| = k|Q|/r²Increases linearly inside; decreases as 1/r² outside
Solid metal sphere (equilibrium)r < a: E = 0r ≥ a: |E| = k|Q|/r²Zero inside; decreases as 1/r² outside
Infinite line of charge|E| = |λ|/(2πε₀r)Decreases as 1/r
Infinite insulating charged sheet|E| = |σ|/(2ε₀) on either sideConstant with distance

For charged spheres, the surface value is the exterior limit for a metal sphere. The insulator field is continuous at r = a. For a negative charge distribution, field direction reverses.
