Three clear calculation steps

    Remember: The field of each ideal infinite sheet has magnitude |σ|/(2ε₀). For a positive sheet, the field points away from the sheet; for a negative sheet, it points toward it. Add the signed x-components.

    Try the lecture examples

    Use the presets to reproduce two important results from the lecture.

    Example A · Oppositely charged sheets (pages 19–20)

    For equal densities +σ and −σ, the electric fields add between the sheets and cancel outside them.

    Ebetween = σ/ε₀

    Example B · Three sheets (page 22)

    The lecture uses −3σ, +2σ, and −σ from top to bottom. Here they are shown right to left in a horizontal x-diagram: −σ (left), +2σ (middle), −3σ (right). P to their left has Ex = +σ/ε₀.

    For this preset σ = 1 nC/m². The horizontal orientation is a rotated representation of the lecture's vertical arrangement.
