Zero-Force Challenge

Can you find the position where two electric forces cancel? Move the third positive charge and watch both force vectors update live.

1 · Investigate the forces

Fixed source charges: q₂ = +6.00 μC at x = 0 and q₁ = +15.0 μC at x = 2.00 m. The movable test charge is q₃ = +1.00 μC for this simulation. Its value is arbitrary and does not change the balance position.

Positive force points right (+x); negative force points left (−x). q₃ stays strictly between the fixed charges so the forces oppose each other. Numerical accuracy uses unrounded values.

2 · Predict before revealing

Where should a third positive charge experience zero net electric force?

3 · Worked solution

At equilibrium, the oppositely directed forces on q₃ have equal magnitudes:

F₂₃ = F₁₃
k |q₂q₃| / x² = k |q₁q₃| / (2.00 − x)²
6.00 / x² = 15.0 / (2.00 − x)²
Reveal the algebra and numerical answer

Cancel k and |q₃|, then take the positive square roots (both distances are positive):

√6.00 / x = √15.0 / (2.00 − x)
(2.00 − x)√6.00 = x√15.0
x = 2.00√6.00 / (√6.00 + √15.0)
x = 0.775 m

This is the only balance point between these two positive source charges. It is closer to the smaller charge, q₂.
