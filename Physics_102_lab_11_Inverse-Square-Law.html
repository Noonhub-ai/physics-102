4 · Make a prediction

With both charges kept constant and nonzero, how does the force magnitude change when the separation is tripled?

Why?

F = k|q₁q₂|/r²
F(3r) = k|q₁q₂|/(3r)² = F(r)/9

Changing the signs of the charges changes attraction or repulsion, but does not change the force magnitude when |q₁|, |q₂|, and r stay fixed.
