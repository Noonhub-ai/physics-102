## Overview

Physics 102 is a set of interactive, browser-based laboratories supporting the study of electric forces and electric fields. The activities are designed to make abstract electrostatics concepts visible and explorable through parameter changes, diagrams, and guided investigation.

The collection is intended to supplement undergraduate instruction and can be accessed through a web browser, including on mobile devices.

## Learning objectives

Through interactive exploration, students can:

- Examine how electric force depends on charge and separation.
- Interpret the vector direction and magnitude of electric forces.
- Apply superposition to forces and electric fields.
- Explore electric fields associated with different charge distributions.
- Interpret electric field lines and charged-particle motion.
- Compare observations from simulations with analytical physics equations.

## How to use

1. Open the [live Physics 102 website](https://noonhub-ai.github.io/physics-102/).
2. Select a laboratory from the chapter index.
3. Follow its instructions, explore the available controls, and observe the diagrams.
4. Use the guided questions and calculations to assess understanding.

The published laboratories run directly in a browser and do not require installation or a GitHub account.
