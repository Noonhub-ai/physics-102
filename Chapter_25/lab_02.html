Does the Path Matter?

Travel from A to B along three different routes in a uniform electric field. Discover why the electric potential difference depends only on the endpoints, not the distance traveled or the shape of the path.

The path integral of a uniform electric field depends on the net horizontal displacement. A vertical part contributes no potential difference because E · ds = 0 there.

4. Discover equipotential lines

The vertical dashed lines in the diagram are equipotential lines. Moving vertically along one of these lines gives Δx = 0, so ΔV = 0.
