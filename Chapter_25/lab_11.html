Conducting Sphere: Potential & Electric Field

Explore an isolated charged metal sphere in electrostatic equilibrium. Discover why the field is zero throughout its interior, while the entire conducting sphere stays at one electric potential.

r < R: V = kQ/R, E = 0   ·   r > R: V = kQ/r, Er = kQ/r²
