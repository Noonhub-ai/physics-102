Potential Difference in a Uniform Electric Field

Drag points A and B between two charged plates. Explore how the direction of displacement determines whether the potential increases, decreases, or stays the same.

ΔV = VB − VA = −EΔx = −Es cos θ

Drag either point, or use the position sliders. The orange plate is positive, the blue plate is negative, and the electric field points to the right. The colored regions and vertical dotted lines show relative potential.

Experiment controls

2. Observe the geometry

θ is measured between the +x electric field direction and the displacement vector from A to B. A vertical displacement is perpendicular to E and contributes zero to ΔV.

3. Make a prediction

As a positive test charge moves from A to B, what happens to the electric potential V?

This question asks about electric potential (V), not potential energy (U). Electric potential is independent of the moving test charge.
