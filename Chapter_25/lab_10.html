Potential Along a Uniformly Charged Ring

Move an observation point along the central axis of a uniformly charged ring. See how the radius, charge, and position affect electric potential and the axial electric field, and why symmetry makes the field zero at the center.

V(x) = kQ / √(R²+x²)   ·   Ex(x) = kQx / (R²+x²)3/2
