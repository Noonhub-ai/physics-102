Electric Potential Superposition

Explore two fixed source charges, q₁ and q₂. Drag point P and change both charges. Find the potential from each charge separately, then add the signed scalar values.

V(P) = V₁ + V₂ = kq₁/r₁ + kq₂/r₂

1. Move the observation point P

Drag P anywhere in the plane (except very close to the source charges). q₁ and q₂ are fixed at x = −1 m and +1 m. Dashed lines show the actual distances r₁ and r₂. Positions and distances are drawn to scale.

2. Adjust the charges and P

Important: Electric potential is a scalar. The potentials V₁ and V₂ add algebraically. Do not resolve them into x and y components.

3. Calculate the total potential

The bars use a common signed scale for the current configuration. Zero is the dotted line in the center.
